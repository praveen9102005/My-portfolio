Smart Agri E-Marketplace with Logistics Integration

Web DevelopmentDatabaseLogistics Integration
1 / 4

Overview

Marketplace that connects farmers with buyers and books transport in the same flow.

Problem

Farmers lose margin to middlemen and struggle to arrange transport for produce.

Solution

A direct listing marketplace with order management and integrated logistics booking.

Key features

  • Farmer product listings
  • Buyer ordering and tracking
  • Logistics booking per order
  • Order and payment status views

System architecture

Farmer→Marketplace→Database→Logistics→Buyer

Challenges

Modelling orders and shipments so both stay in sync.

Future improvements

Price forecasting and mandi-rate insights.
